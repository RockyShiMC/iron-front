Engineers capture enemy buildings and the map's Oil Derricks (steady income), fix your own buildings and rebuild destroyed bridges. Infantry can occupy civilian buildings and fire from inside. Drag to lay a line of walls. Superweapons and support powers appear above Repair / Sell once built.

Build order: Power → Ore Refinery → Barracks → War Factory → Radar. Radar unlocks the Airfield; a Refinery unlocks the Shipyard (build it on water). Aircraft can only be hit by anti-air: rifles, rockets, AA Batteries and some ships. Jets return to an Airfield to rearm.
